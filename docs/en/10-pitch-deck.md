# 10 Pitch deck

[Bahasa Indonesia](../id/10-pitch-deck.md) · **English**

Eleven slides, about five minutes. The demo is the most important slide, so it must run on realistic sample data.

1. **Title:** "Telisik: money moves only when the numbers are true."
2. **Problem:** a Rp10,000 bottle reported as Rp500,000. eFishery reportedly misled investors with revenue 4.8x larger than real. Socks at Rp100k a pair passed through a state budget. Same pattern: money flows, reports go unverified.
3. **Why now:** AI can now read financial documents into structured data. Investment money increasingly moves in stablecoins. Monad is fast and cheap, so every check can be recorded onchain.
4. **Solution:** four modules (report verification, price verification, tranche escrow, audit trail).
5. **Demo:** honest report, tranche released. Marked-up report (bottles at 50x market), tranche frozen. Sock procurement flagged at +200% of the market median.
6. **How it works:** diagram from [03 Architecture](03-architecture.md).
7. **Market:** crypto VCs, then ecosystem grants, then traditional VCs and state-owned CVCs, then investment banks and public procurement.
8. **Business model:** subscription per fund plus an escrow fee per tranche.
9. **Competitors:** the gap table (collecting numbers vs verifying them and locking funds).
10. **Roadmap and ask:** pilot partners, a Monad grant or residency, legal advisors.
11. **Team:** AI/ML and blockchain background, plus roles still needed (e.g. finance or audit expert).

## Opening line

> "A Rp10,000 bottle shows up in a report as Rp500,000. eFishery reportedly misled investors for years. Socks at Rp100k a pair passed through a state budget. Same problem: money flows, reports go unverified. Telisik checks every report and every price against reality, and the money moves only when they check out. The record lives permanently on Monad."

## Notes for Monad judges

- Mention Danantara only as a long-term market, never as the first customer.
- Be ready for "why Monad, not Ethereum?": cheap attestations, 0.6s finality, native USDC and an official x402 facilitator, and standard EVM contracts that can move.
- Do not claim Telisik would have stopped eFishery if the demo only shows price checks. Revenue and asset checks are on the roadmap.
