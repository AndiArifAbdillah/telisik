# 01 Problem

[Bahasa Indonesia](../id/01-masalah.md) · **English**

Investor and public money moves on reports that nobody checks against reality, so fraud surfaces only after the loss.

## Three cases

| Case | What happened | Missing check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [eFishery](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/efishery-forensic-audit-inflated-revenue-427198) (private investors) | Allegedly kept two sets of books since 2018. Jan–Sep 2024 revenue was reported at about US$750M while internal records showed about US$161M (4.8x). It claimed 400,000 fish feeders; about 24,000 existed. Investors included SoftBank, Temasek and Sequoia India. | Report vs source data (bank, sales, assets) |
| [Socks bought by BGN for the MBG free-meals program](https://wartaekonomi.co.id/read607640/bukan-menu-makan-kaos-kaki-rp100-ribu-justru-jadi-prioritas-mbg) (public budget) | About Rp6.9B of socks according to LKPP procurement data, at up to Rp100k per pair (17,000 pairs of field socks worth Rp1.7B). Criticized as far above market prices. | Line-item price vs market price |
| [TaniHub](https://indonesiabusinesspost.com/7051/corporate-affairs/telkommetra-weighs-mdi-ventures-divestment-amid-danantara-overhaul) (state-owned corporate VCs) | A court convicted four former executives of MDI Ventures and BRI Ventures over a joint US$25M investment in TaniHub, which later collapsed. | Proof that fund managers verified and monitored |

## Important notes

- The eFishery findings come from a forensic audit reported by the media, not a court ruling.
- The head of BGN denied that the sock procurement value was inflated, and there is no official audit finding of a markup.
- Telisik accuses no one. Its output is a gap, the evidence, and a request for clarification.

## The same pattern

| | eFishery | MBG socks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type of problem | Report does not match reality | Price far above market |
| Check needed | Report matched against source data | Line-item price compared with market price |
| Who loses | Investors | Taxpayers |
